Our Vision

To make disciples of all nations, raising leaders and giving hope to the hopeless.

One vision shapes every service, every ministry and every step we take as a church family.

01

Proclaim the Gospel

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

02

Preach and Teach

To preach and teach the Word of God all over the world.

03

Peace and Reconciliation

To promote the Gospel of peace and reconciliation in Jesus Christ and demonstrate His healing power.

04

Holy Ghost Power

To demonstrate the power of the Holy Ghost, liberating men from the oppression of the devil and his agents.

Word. Spirit. Power.
