Questions

A few things you may be wondering.

What should I expect on my first visit?

You will find a warm welcome, sincere worship, practical Bible teaching and a family that is glad you came. Come as you are — there is no dress code and no pressure.

Where do I park?

Please contact the church office for current parking guidance and directions. Our team will be happy to help you plan your visit.

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Children are valued and cared for in our Children's Church. Let us know you are coming and we will help your family settle in.

How can I request prayer?

Use the contact form above, call the church office or speak with a member of our team at any gathering. We would be honoured to pray with you.
