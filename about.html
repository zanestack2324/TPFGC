Our story

Rooted in the Word. Alive in the Spirit.

We are a Christ-centred family with a clear calling: to preach the true Gospel, make disciples of all nations, raise leaders and give hope to the hopeless.

A family of faith.

Welcome home

A church where faith becomes family.

We are a Spirit-filled family gathered around the Word of God, passionate about seeing lives transformed by the power of Jesus Christ.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time, returning to church after a long season, or looking for a place to grow and serve, there is a place for you here. We believe the Gospel is good news for every person, every family and every nation.

Raising leaders. Giving hope to the hopeless. Making disciples of all nations.
Our calling in one sentence

Our Vision

To make disciples of all nations, raising leaders and giving hope to the hopeless.

Our Mission

Four commitments that shape everything we do.

01

Proclaim the Gospel

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

02

Preach and Teach

To preach and teach the Word of God all over the world.

03

Peace and Reconciliation

To promote the Gospel of peace and reconciliation in Jesus Christ and demonstrate His healing power.

04

Holy Ghost Power

To demonstrate the power of the Holy Ghost, liberating men from the oppression of the devil and his agents.

Our Aims & Objectives

Eight ways we say yes to God.

These God-given objectives guide our worship, our witness and our work — from the pulpit to the nations.

01

Preach and Teach the Word

To preach and teach the Word of God all over the world.

02

Proclaim the True Gospel

To proclaim the true Gospel of Jesus Christ.

03

Crusades and Seminars

To organize crusades, seminars and other gatherings that advance the Kingdom of God.

04

Peace and Reconciliation

To promote the Gospel of peace and reconciliation in Jesus Christ and demonstrate His healing power.

05

Establish Branches

To establish branches for the expansion of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

06

Make Disciples

To make disciples of all nations, raising leaders and giving hope to the hopeless.

07

Demonstrate the Holy Ghost

To demonstrate the power of the Holy Ghost, liberating men from the oppression of the devil and his agents.

08

Promote Unity

To promote unity among Christians by de-emphasizing those things that divide us.

What anchors us

Values that shape our life together.

The Gospel is not only something we preach. It is something we live, together.

Word-centred

Scripture shapes our teaching, our decisions and our daily walk with God.

Spirit-led

We make room for the presence and power of the Holy Ghost in every gathering.

People-first

Every person is seen, valued and invited to grow in grace and purpose.

Mission-minded

We carry the Gospel beyond our walls with compassion, courage and action.
