Partnership

Partner with the mission.

If you would like to give, partner monthly or support a specific area of ministry, we would love to hear from you.

How do I get verified giving details?

Please contact the church office by phone or email. Our team will share the current verified bank transfer details and answer any questions you have.

Can I give to a specific ministry or project?

Yes. Let us know the ministry or project you would like to support and our team will guide you on how to give with a clear designation.

Can I set up a recurring partnership?

We would be glad to help you set up a monthly partnership. Contact us and we will walk you through the available options.

Will my giving be acknowledged?

Yes. Please include your name and contact details when you give so that we can acknowledge your gift and provide a receipt where appropriate.
